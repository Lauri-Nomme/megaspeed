### Cross-check
- **Sprint 2 membership/done-by-end: Right.** 30 stories (112/118/125 pts for S1/S2/S3), done by 7 Oct 16:00: **15** (52 of 118 pts = **44 % by points**), late 12, never 3 — exact. - **"2 committed / 28 adopted" is a strict-timestamp artefact: Misleading.** `jira._added_at_start` counts a story as committed only if `sprint_added ≤ sprint start` (09:00:00). In S2, **2 stories are added at 09:00:00 and 3 more at 09:30** (S1: 7 within 8 h of start; S3: 7). The next S2 add is 3 d 4 h later. Realistic "plan-day" commitment is 5 (S2), not 2; "28 adopted mid-sprint" is overstated by 3-5 stories in every sprint, and the headline of the #1-ranked critical rule is built on it. Also *"(44 % by points)"* is the **done** share by points (52/118), but it is attached to "28 adopted" and reads as "adopted = 44 %"; adopted points are 111/118 = 94 %.
- **"Did S2 deliver" understates team output.** Stories actually *finished inside S2's dates (11 Sep 09:00 → 7 Oct 16:00)*: **23 stories / 84 pts** — 15 of S2's own plus **8 S1 carry-overs** that finished during S2 (they are credited to nobody: S1 shows "8 late", S2 shows nothing, "27 never reappear in the next sprint"). The lens answers "did S2's members finish?" (no) but not "how much did the team ship in S2?" (23). No velocity per sprint window.
- **Round cap**: r(rounds, cycle) over 106 merged PRs = 0.596 (product 0.61/0.62 on its definition) ✓; but the card's "measured lever, not a style preference" is a correlation (rounds and cycle co-vary by construction). The lab's −13 % is likewise built by removing the rounds above the cap.
- **Refinement**: story-level created→ready = 27.7 % of summed cycle across 87 done stories (product 30 % on its 85-story window; review-rounds segment 31.6 %, i.e. tied). Median 26.7 h ✓. The lab cannot model it ("added work on the PR clock") and returns **+82 %**.
- **Smaller PRs**: r(commits, cycle) = 0.158, r(lines, cycle) = 0.196 (✓ 0.16/0.20); the lab's +289 % is because every split PR needs the single mandatory sentinel again (sentinel → 122 % busy). Rule #09 still recommends "smaller PRs".
- **Second sentinel**: lab −3.0 % ("no clear effect"); rule #10 still ranks it critical ("Add a second sentinel or a rotation"). It is the sentinel-*tail* (2 PRs, 16-19 d) that is a real problem, which the lab states it cannot see.

### Verdict
- **Accuracy: Right** for every number; the lab disclosures are honest ("not a measured win", "cannot see the forgotten-item tail").
- **Helpfulness: weak on "first".** The page gives ten cards in an order driven by "impact × confidence" with assumed impacts (#08 "assumed"), a banner naming a different best lever than card #1, and three cards (#08, #09, #10) whose primary advice is contradicted by the lab's verdict printed on the same card. ## Did it get better, and what still grinds

**Better than my expectation of a v0.10 product** — the three headline questions are answerable on screen: "Done in Jira, PR open" is a first-class list, the ticket page benchmarks and names the 60 h no-PR gap, the repo scope really threads through every view and reconciles to the team total, the Trend/Rework/Sprint-at-risk surfaces exist and their numbers hold up (I re-derived >60 numbers; none of the headline totals was wrong), the lab prints its limits ("not a measured win"), the data-quirk clamp replaced out-of-order segment sums, and the Timeline scoped to a ticket (`08-timeline-167.png`) is excellent for "show me this story". Back-navigation/YAH, error states and the calendar assumption line are exemplary.

**What still grinds** — the product is accurate but not decisive, and the recurring theme survives: **the same concept reads differently in different views.** "Wait on Frank" is 6 h / 75 d / 165 d; "mean wait" is 6 h 40 vs 11.1 h on one screen; "orphaned approval" includes PRs that have no approval; "cycle" is PR clock in Trend/Lab/Delivery and ticket clock on the ticket page; "committed" is a to-the-second timestamp; "late" counts carry-overs against the old sprint while the new sprint gets no credit; "latest" in Trend is sometimes the empty bucket. Three cards on the Rules page (and the chase card's SLA link) recommend what the lab, printed on the same card, says does not work, so a lead still has to run the lab by hand to find the −22.6 % combination. The new surfaces are lists (at-risk, rework, chase) where a lead wants verdicts, owners and forecasts. Layout debt at 1600 px (clipped "who owes" columns, run-on carry-over text, broken worklog bars) makes the most important cells the hardest to read.

Highest-leverage next fixes: (1) one stated constraint verdict reconciling the sentinel clocks and removing the 5×-peer people-level number from the headline; (2) redefine "orphaned approval" to PRs with all peer approvals in and exclude not-yet-his-turn PRs from FrMo's "never"; (3) plan-day grace for "committed" and a velocity-per-window line; (4) fix the worklog bars and the Trend "latest" rule; (5) call out reopen loops and no-PR gaps by their own names in the decomposition; (6) make the Rules cards' primary advice agree with the lab verdict printed beneath it.
